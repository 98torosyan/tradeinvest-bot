Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TradeInvest (@armtradeinvest) publishes educational trading content to its own Instagram and YouTube accounts. The "TradeInvest Uploader" application is used only by the account owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

The application does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other users. It accesses only the owner's channel with the youtube.upload scope, and the access token is stored as an encrypted secret used solely for uploads.

This website does not use advertising cookies. Basic, privacy-friendly page statistics may be counted without identifying visitors.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
